<!-- feature-intro -->
Give authors visual, self-explanatory choices without turning your templates into a guessing game. Button Box keeps every stored value stable while the Craft control panel shows what that value actually means.
<!-- feature-intro-end -->

<!-- feature-section media-size="large" -->
## Make the right choice obvious

Present the same approved values as separate buttons, button groups, chips, dropdowns, lists, tiles, cards, swatches or visual samples. Add a useful description, familiar icon, image, colour or surface preview so authors can recognise the choice instead of memorising an internal token.

Use Visual Choice for one selection or Visual Multi-Choice when authors need to choose a set, such as topics or content features.

![Story layout cards with Editorial selected beside Gallery and Feature, each with an icon and description](../screenshots/fields/choice-cards.png)
<!-- feature-section-end -->

## Let authors see the palette

“Coast” means more when authors can see the colours. Present solid colours, split swatches, foreground and background pairs, or gradients as recognisable choices. Keep the selected value stable while giving your templates access to the visual metadata behind it.

![Colour theme choices showing Ocean, Dusk, Ink and paper, and the selected Coast gradient](../screenshots/fields/compound-surfaces.png)
<!-- feature-media-end -->

## Set the choices once, use them where they belong

A shared palette or approved set of card treatments should stay consistent across your fields. Manage named option sets in the control panel, edit them from a field's slide-out, and deploy them through project config. When a field needs to go its own way, convert its options to an independent copy.

![Visual Choice field using the Brand surfaces option set, with New, Edit and Convert to Custom controls above its preview](../screenshots/settings/shared-options.png)
<!-- feature-media-end -->

<!-- feature-media -->
## Start with a preset, make it your own

For reusable starting points, create your own presets or customise the built-in visual, position, width and rating presets. Applying one copies its settings into a field, so later preset edits leave existing fields as you configured them.

Start with an alignment picker, a typography scale or a set of shape samples, then adjust the choices and appearance to suit your project. Live previews let you see the result as you configure the field.

![Preset chooser showing alignment, spacing, colour, aspect ratio, typography, image fit, shape and surface options alongside a custom Coastal palette](../screenshots/settings/visual-presets.png)
<!-- feature-media-end -->

<!-- feature-section media-size="small" -->
## Put the next step within reach

Give editors a direct route to the story they are working on or the brand guidelines they need. Reuse conditional Action Links across field layouts, with shared labels, icons, images and button styles. Links can use the current entry to build their destination without storing executable JavaScript.

![Editorial shortcuts with View story and Brand guidelines buttons](../screenshots/fields/action-links.png)
<!-- feature-section-end -->

<!-- feature-section -->
## Keep frontend decisions in the frontend

Button Box improves authoring without shipping opinions into your public site. Twig and GraphQL expose stable values and typed choice and position metadata; your project decides how those values affect components, classes, tokens and responsive behaviour.
<!-- feature-section-end -->

<!-- feature-grid -->
- :icon[code] **Project-driven options** Supply choices through PHP when the available options depend on your application.
- :icon[table-options] **Craft-native administration** Keep labels and values together in compact rows, expand the details when needed, and compare presentations before saving.
- :icon[filter] **First-class Craft behaviour** Work with your selections in element previews, search, conditions, sorting and inline editing, with support suited to each content field.
<!-- feature-grid-end -->

<!-- feature-section -->
## Keep existing content, adopt richer choices

Your existing Button Box fields retain their settings, stored values and template behaviour. Adopt richer choices where they help, without converting every field as part of an update.

When you are ready to convert a field, preview the conversion, see blockers and warnings, and review the appearance and API changes before opting in.
